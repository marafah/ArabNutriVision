{% translate "The gap" %}

{% translate "Food-image systems have learned to name Middle Eastern dishes with high accuracy, but none reports a validated nutrient error for Levantine composite dishes. In these dishes the energy-dense components (frying oil, ghee, tahini, fatty meat) are mixed into or hidden beneath rice and bread. A camera sees the dish; it cannot see how it was cooked. Generic multimodal AI models therefore fail silently: recent evaluations report the largest errors for fat and for complex meals." %}
